## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, editorial sanctuary for high-end urban living in Ho Chi Minh City. Moving away from the chaotic visual noise, dense banners, and garish badges of conventional Southeast Asian classified listings, the aesthetic evokes the quiet luxury of an architectural monograph or contemporary design quarterly.

The brand persona balances curated prestige with analytical rigor. It speaks to discerning urban professionals, expatriates, and design-conscious locals who view housing not as sheer square footage, but as sanctuary and personal expression. 

### Visual Expression
- **Atmospheric Clarity:** Pure white canvas punctuated by warm limestone washes (`#FAF8F5`), high whitespace ratios, and disciplined vertical rhythms.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines paired with precise, human-centered sans-serif typography engineered specifically for Vietnamese tonal diacritics.
- **Understated Precision:** Structural separation relies on whisper-thin hairline rules and airy ambient elevations rather than container stacking or heavy cards.
- **Iconography & Motif:** Clean, 1.5px stroke geometric line art. The lighthouse emblem serves as an understated beam of guidance through the dense urban fabric of Saigon.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the juxtaposition of tropical botanical serenity and classical French colonial architecture in Saigon: aged stone, deep shaded verandas, and understated champagne brass accents.

### Color Tokens
- **Background Primary (`#FFFFFF`):** Dominant surface canvas providing museum-grade airiness.
- **Background Secondary (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm limestone off-white used for alternate editorial sections, filter bars, and modal underlays.
- **Text Ink (`#111827`):** Deep carbon neutral for authoritative text contrast.
- **Text Muted (`#6B7280`):** Neutral gray for secondary captions, metadata, and directional microcopy.
- **Hairline Border (`#E7E2DA`):** 1px structural separator preserving visual order without adding bulk.
- **Deep Teal (`#0F5F58` - Primary):** Anchor tone for interactive selections, focus rings, interactive toggles, and property verification markers.
- **Champagne Gold (`#B8892E` - Secondary / Accent):** Reserved for high-value key actions, median benchmarks, verified architectural seals, and luxury tiers.
- **Benchmark Favorable (`#047857` - Success):** Understated forest tone indicating rent favorable to market medians ("Rẻ hơn ước tính" / "Giá tốt").
- **Benchmark Elevated (`#B91C1C` - Danger):** Controlled crimson reserved for price points exceeding market averages ("Cao hơn ước tính").

## Typography

Typography bridges timeless print editorial with contemporary screen precision. 

### Font Rules & Vietnamese Language Support
- **Headline Font (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for atmospheric storytelling, location names (e.g., *Thảo Điền*, *Bến Nghé*), editorial room titles, and hero titles. The high contrast of Playfair Display gracefully carries Vietnamese tone marks (dấu hỏi, ngã, nặng, sắc, huyền) without glyph collision when using generous line heights.
- **Body & Numeric Font (`Be Vietnam Pro`):** Engineered natively for Vietnamese diacritics, avoiding unnatural diacritic shifts or clipping. 
- **Tabular Numerals:** All financial figures, room dimensions (m²), and coordinates must enforce tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1;`).
- **Currency Styling:** Price values are treated as structural visual anchors. Numbers are rendered bold and dark (`#111827`), followed by the currency symbol and billing period in muted tone (`₫/tháng` in `#6B7280`).

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses a responsive 12-column grid system on desktop (max content container: 1320px) transitioning to a 4-column structure on mobile devices.

### Layout Principles
- **Editorial White Space:** Generous vertical rhythm separates major discovery clusters. Avoid dense multi-row card clusters; favor 2 to 3 cards per row on desktop to give each listing room to breathe.
- **Asymmetric Anchors:** Alternating pure `#FFFFFF` backdrops with edge-to-edge `#FAF8F5` banded sections for market insights, curated neighbourhood essays, and property collections.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, 48px canvas margin, 24px gutter.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 32px canvas margin, 20px gutter.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px canvas margin, 16px gutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes surface purity, soft natural light, and structural planar boundaries over heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Flat Surface Tier:** Default resting state for cards, lists, and form regions, using a 1px solid hairline (`#E7E2DA`) without cast shadow.
- **Floating Nav & Modals:** Soft, highly-diffuse ambient depth:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.06);`
- **Active Card Hover:** A delicate lift combining border illumination and subtle feathering:
  `box-shadow: 0 14px 36px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08); transform: translateY(-2px);`
- **Backdrop Blur / Scrim:** Navigation headers and sticky price bars utilize a frosted milky wash:
  `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88); backdrop-filter: blur(12px);`

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette combines softened corners with tailored pill silhouettes for an approachable yet high-end architectural character.

### Curvature Guidelines
- **Property Cards:** Fixed 20px radius (`border-radius: 20px;`) with overflow clip on photo galleries.
- **Search Command Bar:** Fully rounded pill (`border-radius: 9999px;`) communicating smooth, frictionless entry.
- **Pills, Badges & Chips:** Fully rounded (`border-radius: 9999px;`) to contrast cleanly with rectangular photographic layouts.
- **Input Fields & Dialogue Panels:** 12px to 16px radius, offering a disciplined, structured feel that anchors text.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Champagne Gold):** Background `#B8892E`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight 600. For key transactional actions: "Đặt lịch xem phòng", "Gửi yêu cầu thuê".
- **Secondary Action (Deep Teal Ghost / Outline):** 1px border `#0F5F58`, text `#0F5F58`, transparent background. For ancillary actions: "Lưu tin", "Xem vị trí bản đồ".
- **Tertiary Minimal:** Transparent background, text `#111827`, subtle underline on hover. Used for navigation and text links.

### 2. Search Bar (Global Architectural Pill)
- Centered, floating pill-form (`border-radius: 9999px`) styled with `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px solid `#E7E2DA`, and ambient shadow `0 10px 30px rgba(17,24,39,0.06)`.
- Divided internally into three segments via vertical hairline dividers:
  1. *Khu vực / Quận* (e.g., Quận 1, Quận 3, Bình Thạnh).
  2. *Mức ngân sách* (e.g., 10 - 25 tr ₫).
  3. *Loại không gian* (e.g., Studio, Penthouse, Duplex).
- Trailing circular search button in `#0F5F58` with a 1.5px minimal search icon.

### 3. Property Cards
- Outer boundary: 20px border-radius, 1px border `#E7E2DA`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Photography: 4:3 or 16:10 aspect ratio with an internal 16px inset or edge-to-edge flush top with 20px top corner radius.
- Price Display: Bold, large Be Vietnam Pro font (`14.500.000 ₫/tháng`) positioned prominently above bedroom and bathroom metadata.
- Verification Marker: Minimal pill badge top-left of image: `#FAF8F5` background, `#0F5F58` text and verified checkmark.

### 4. Signature Component: "Dải Giá Thị Trường" (Price Range Strip)
- **Concept:** A clean quantitative widget embedded in listing cards and detail headers that provides transparent rental context.
- **Geometry:** A 4px high continuous rounded track (`#E7E2DA`) representing the 25th to 75th percentile market rent for the specific sub-district.
- **Track Range:** The interquartile range (p25–p75) is highlighted with a subtle `#FAF8F5` or muted teal tone.
- **Median Marker:** A 10px circular Champagne Gold dot (`#B8892E`) pinned at the neighborhood median.
- **Current Price Marker:** A 14px circular Deep Teal indicator (`#0F5F58`) with a white inner core positioned at the current listing's rate.
- **Benchmark Evaluation Tag:** Positioned directly above the indicator:
  - Favorable: Light pill `#ECFDF5`, text `#047857` ("Rẻ hơn 12% so với trung vị").
  - Above Median: Light pill `#FEF2F2`, text `#B91C1C` ("Cao hơn 8% so với trung vị").

### 5. Chips & Filter Tags
- Height: 36px, `border-radius: 9999px`.
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E7E2DA` border, text `#6B7280`.
- Active: `#0F5F58` background, text `#FFFFFF`, border-color `#0F5F58`.

### 6. Input Fields
- Inset padding: 14px 18px. Radius: 12px.
- Resting: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E7E2DA` border, text `#111827`.
- Focus: Border `#0F5F58`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 95, 88, 0.12)`.
- Helper / Error Labels: Rendered in 13px font with dedicated vertical spacing to prevent jumpy layout shifts.
